From one signal to a proven fix.

What happens between a metric crossing a line and your regulator report.

1A signal arrivesFrom any connector, by webhook or by polling
2It is groupedWith every other signal from the same failure
3Vigil reads around itRecent changes, metrics and logs, through the same connectors
4A fix is approvedAnd run through the connector that can act, inside your cloud
5Evidence is sealedEvery read and every action, in the hash chained log

No connector yet? Build your own.

The connector kit turns any HTTP API into a Relyxus connector, from a short definition or straight from its OpenAPI file.

card_processor.yamlConnector kit
name: card_processor
base_url: https://api.processor.example
auth: bearer_token   # kept in your secrets store
queries:
  - name: authorisation_errors
    path: /v1/stats/errors
    params: [region, since]
  - name: status
    path: /v1/status
Connected: card_processor

Two queries available to Vigil and to your runbooks.
