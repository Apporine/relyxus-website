Who we are

This website is run by Apporine, the company behind Relyxus. For this website and our sales conversations, Apporine is the controller of your personal data. Our company details will appear here once registration is complete.

What we collect

When you book a demo or ask for documents: your name, work email, company, role and anything you choose to tell us. When you send an incident for replay: the files you choose to share. When you chat with the Relyxus assistant: what you type or say, and any details you share in the chat. When you browse: basic technical information needed to run the site securely.

Why we use it

To answer you, arrange and run demos, prepare replays and send the documents you asked for. Our lawful basis is your request, or our legitimate interest in replying to business enquiries.

The Relyxus assistant

The assistant on this website is an AI. When you chat or switch on voice, what you type or say is sent to Google, which runs the AI for us, so it can reply. Google may use what you send to improve its services, so please do not share anything confidential in the chat. If you give us your name and email, we keep the conversation with your details so the team can follow up. The assistant can make mistakes. Anything it says about pricing, contracts or rules is confirmed in writing by the team.

Who we share it with

Only the providers that run this website and our work for us: Vercel, which hosts the website, and Google, which runs the assistant and where we keep enquiries and replay files (Google Sheets, Drive and Gmail). Some of these providers process data outside the UK, with the safeguards UK law requires. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details for as long as we are talking, and then for a limited time. Replay files are deleted when you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or restrict your data, object to how we use it or ask for a copy. Email support@relyxus.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
