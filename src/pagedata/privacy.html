Who we are

This website is run by Apporine, the company behind Relyxus. For this website and our sales conversations, Apporine is the controller of your personal data. Our company details will appear here once registration is complete.

What we collect

When you book a demo or ask for documents: your name, work email, company, role and anything you choose to tell us. When you send an incident for replay: the files you choose to share. When you browse: basic technical information needed to run the site securely.

Why we use it

To answer you, arrange and run demos, prepare replays and send the documents you asked for. Our lawful basis is your request, or our legitimate interest in replying to business enquiries.

Who we share it with

Only the providers that host this website and our email. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details for as long as we are talking, and then for a limited time. Replay files are deleted when you ask.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or restrict your data, object to how we use it or ask for a copy. Email support@relyxus.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
